BUG: README.md shows times such as "8:30am", and the program intends to print times that way
(it replaces " AM" by "am" and " PM" by "pm").  But under JDK 20 and later, the time formatter
puts U+202F NARROW NO-BREAK SPACE, not an ordinary space, before "AM" and "PM", so the program
prints times such as "8:30<U+202F>AM".  The goal files of every test case record this behavior.
